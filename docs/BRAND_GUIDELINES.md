# PVNetwork Brand Guidelines

The official PVNetwork identity is the user-supplied gold/black `PRIVATE NETWORK` square artwork: gold shield/keyhole, three stars, orbit ring and PRIVATE NETWORK wordmark.

## Rules
- Use the supplied artwork as source of truth.
- Never replace it with a generic shield, Flutter icon, AI-generated substitute or competitor asset.
- Do not distort, recolor or change proportions.
- Technical derivatives are allowed for launcher, TV, iOS/macOS, Windows, Linux, tray/notification and splash assets.
- For very small icons, a shield-centered crop may be derived from the same supplied artwork; the full artwork remains on splash/about/brand surfaces.
- Light theme must place the unchanged artwork on a suitable dark brand surface rather than recoloring it.

- official PVNetwork artwork in-app
- launcher/desktop/TV icons derived from it
